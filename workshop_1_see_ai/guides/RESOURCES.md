# 📚 Workshop 1 — Learning Resources

Curated resources to deepen your understanding after completing Workshop 1.

---

## 🎓 Foundational Courses (Start Here)

### 1. Google's Generative AI Learning Path
- **Link**: [Google Cloud Skills Boost](https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/paths/118)
- Short, easy-to-digest modules covering intro to LLMs, prompt design, Transformer models, and image generation.

### 2. Hugging Face NLP Course
- **Link**: [Hugging Face NLP Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course/chapter1/1)
- The official course from the creators of the `transformers` library we used in this workshop. Entirely free and hands-on.

### 3. DeepLearning.AI — Generative AI for Everyone
- **Link**: [Generative AI for Everyone](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/generative-ai-for-everyone/)
-  Taught by Andrew Ng. Less technical, focuses on building intuition about what GenAI can do and its societal impact.

### 4. Google GenAI Intensive Courses
- [5-Day Gen AI Intensive Course](https://www.kaggle.com/learn-guide/5-day-genai) — Comprehensive GenAI foundations with whitepapers.
- [5-Day AI Agents Intensive Course](https://www.kaggle.com/learn-guide/5-day-agents) — Deep dive into the agentic AI space.

---

## 🛠️ Hands-On Platforms

- **[Transformer Explainer](https://poloclub.github.io/transformer-explainer/)** — Interactive visualization of the Transformer architecture. The best way to build intuition.
- **[Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/models)** — Discover models for any task (text, image, audio). Try them directly in "Spaces".
- **[Kaggle Notebooks](https://www.kaggle.com/code)** — Thousands of real-world AI notebooks with free GPU.
- **[Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com)** — The tool we use in this workshop. Great for experimentation.

---

## 📚 Go Deeper

- **Paper**: ["Attention Is All You Need" (2017)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/3f5ee243547dee91fbd053c1c4a845aa-Paper.pdf) — The foundational paper that introduced the Transformer architecture.
- **Book**: *"Natural Language Processing with Transformers"* — By Hugging Face staff. The definitive guide.
- **Book**: *"Hands-On Large Language Models"* — By Jay Alammar & Maarten Grootendorst. Practical guide to understanding and building LLMs.
- **Book**: *"AI Engineering"* — By Chip Huyen. Production-ready GenAI systems.
